Read it right

It judges your record, not your future.

A high number means your past results are hard to explain by luck. Markets change, and an edge that was real can stop working.

Every setup you tried counts.

Test five ideas and keep the best, and the best one looks good even if none of them works. Each extra idea raises the bar your results have to clear.

Fees count. A breakeven is a loss.

Commissions turn a breakeven trade into a losing one. Leave subtract fees on unless your P&L column already includes them.

The math

The check measures how far your average trade sits above zero relative to how much your trades swing, then asks what the best of your setups would have shown with no edge at all, and holds your results to that bar.

The dashed line on the equity chart is that bar: what the luckiest of your setups would have made by now, at your trade size, with no edge. Finishing above it is the same as a chance above 50%.

The method is the Probabilistic and Deflated Sharpe Ratio of Bailey and López de Prado (2012, 2014). It assumes the setups were independent and tested on a similar number of trades. Tested on simulated traders with no edge, it wrongly called an edge proven about 5% of the time for one setup, and about 1% or less for five or more.

For developers

The same checks, plus the Probability of Backtest Overfitting and purged cross-validation, are in an open-source Python library. Every worked example in the source papers reproduces, and this page is tested against the library.

Export help

Export the closed trades of one account, then filter to one strategy above. Menus change between versions: if yours look different, look for Export on the trades or history view.

TopstepX

Open the Trades tab at the bottom of the platform (not Orders), choose the date range and click Export. If the file has separate fee columns, keep subtract fees on.

Quantower

Open the Trades panel, choose Export data from its menu, tick all columns, pick comma-separated and export. Fills that share a position ID are combined into one trade, using the Net P/L column.

NinjaTrader 8

Control Center, New → Trade Performance, choose the account, strategy and dates, click Generate, open the Trades view, then right-click and Export. Check whether your Profit column already includes commission, and set subtract fees to match.

thinkorswim (Schwab)

Monitor → Account Statement, set the dates, then Export to File from the menu at the top right. The page pairs the buys and sells in Account Trade History into round trips (first in, first out). Commissions are not in that section, so results are before fees.
